# Author agreement templates

The only bundled template is `placeholder-v1`. It is explicitly a prototype workflow text, not approved legal or publication terms. The frozen registry in `app/agreement_content.py` retains its exact version and text and computes a content fingerprint. Historical snapshots that do not exactly match a known registry entry are labeled unverified, not approved. No official template or approval record was found in the inspected project materials.

Before production use, the owner must supply the exact approved agreement text, a unique version identifier, the identity or authority approving it, the approval date and a durable approval reference. A reviewed implementation must preserve that evidence and the exact snapshot, including for older versions. Do not replace it with generated legal clauses. The application currently recognizes development and local demo modes only; it has no supported production mode. Prototype content is not issuable through `issuance_template` for a non-prototype mode. A future approved template also requires migration 034's intact database guards before issuance.

Migration 034 is additive and explicit. It protects agreement snapshot identity/text/version, prevents deletion, prevents changes to finalized response state and timestamp, and validates the Author/manuscript/accept-decision binding on new inserts. It refuses the repository's persistent Main database. It was tested on disposable data only. Prototype workflows remain available on pre-034 development databases for compatibility; those databases do not have database-enforced snapshot immutability. Applying the migration to persistent data requires the separately authorized operational process and backup. Existing agreement records are neither backfilled nor represented as approved.

The original requirement describes a downloadable artifact as optional, so no PDF or DOCX subsystem is added. An Author response uses the stored snapshot version, and a repeated identical response returns the saved result without issuing notifications again. A conflicting response is denied. This post-review agreement is separate from general participant-policy acceptance.

For prospective Final Editor invitations and accepted-invitation editing, the current agreement must be accepted, belong to the manuscript's Author, and refer to its current-round EIC acceptance decision. A manuscript with a recorded Final Editor invitation cannot use the old no-agreement submission fallback. The pre-existing compatibility path for older final-editing records with no agreement and no Final Editor invitation remains; it is not evidence of an accepted agreement. The owner must decide whether and how to migrate or re-consent those historical records before treating them as production agreements.
